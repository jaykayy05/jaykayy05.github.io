Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. This policy covers the Vice City Replay Room desktop uploader and this information site.

Information used by the uploader

When the operator grants access through Google, the uploader reads the authorized YouTube channel ID and checks the IDs, visibility, and processing status of videos it uploads. It sends the operator’s selected video and associated metadata to YouTube to create a private upload. It does not request YouTube Analytics or access to Gmail, contacts, or payment information.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials are stored in the operator’s macOS Keychain. An access token is used in memory to make requests to Google. The uploader stores local upload receipts, including the video ID, to prevent duplicate uploads. It does not send YouTube account data to an analytics service, advertising service, or other recipient. The uploader does not sell Google user data or use it to train AI models.

Google handles sign-in and processes uploads under the Google Privacy Policy. The uploader’s use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access, retention, and deletion

The operator can revoke the uploader’s access in Google Account connections. The operator can remove the locally saved credential items from Keychain and remove upload receipts from the uploader’s local runtime folder. Videos already sent to YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio.

Visits to this website

This information site has no forms, ads, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may log visitor IP addresses for security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact

For questions about the uploader or this policy, use the support contact listed on the app’s Google consent screen.
