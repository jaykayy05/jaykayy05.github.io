Vice City Replay Room

Vice City Replay Room is a desktop uploader used by the operator of the Vice City Replay Room YouTube channel.

What the uploader does

It checks the authorized YouTube channel, uploads approved video files as private videos, and checks the status of those uploads. Google authorization begins from the uploader on the operator’s computer.

Why it asks for YouTube access

  • Read access checks the channel ID and the status of videos the uploader sends.
  • Upload access lets the uploader send a selected video and its title, description, and publishing settings to YouTube.

See the privacy policy for what the uploader stores and how to remove access. The terms describe who can use it.
